# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors should communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical
work, and avoid harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of private data.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may restrict
participation when necessary. Security and privacy concerns should be reported
privately according to `SECURITY.md`.
